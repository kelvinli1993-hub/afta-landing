What Makes Afta Special

Software that acts like a thoughtful friend, not a cold database.

We understand that dealing with loss is heavy. Most administrative software and tasks feel transactional, adding to the exhaustion.

Afta was designed to feel gentle, human, and grounded at every turn—cutting through administrative noise and complexity.

Adaptive roadmaps

Every family’s journey is different. Afta creates a custom, prioritized timeline tailored to your exact province, assets, and estate complexity.

Intelligent dispatch

Pre-draft formal letters, notifications, and statutory applications with built-in templates.

Private & encrypted

Your family’s sensitive details are encrypted locally on your device. We can never see, share, or sell your documents.

Family harmony
